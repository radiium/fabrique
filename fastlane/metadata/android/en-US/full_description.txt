Five calculators for the woodworking shop. Every result updates as you type, with a dimensioned drawing.

<b>Surface layout</b>: lay identical pieces over a surface (tile, boards, flooring, pavers, sheets). Number of whole and cut pieces, cut sizes, waste percentage, stacked or offset joints, gaps and perimeter gap.

<b>Even spacing</b>: space pieces across a width (balusters, slats, shelves, drilling centers). From a count or a target gap, with margins, and the pitch to mark out.

<b>Drawers</b>: from the cabinet opening, the cut list for the boxes and fronts, the slide length and where to screw the slides.

<b>Level</b>: level and plumb with the phone standing on its edge, using its accelerometer. Reversal calibration: the surface does not need to be level.

<b>Converter</b>: lengths, areas, volumes, weights and pressures, limited to the units you meet in the shop. Inches in fractions down to 1/16, board feet.

Each calculation's drawing exports as an image, to your photos or through sharing.

Everything is in millimeters. In French and English. No ads, no account, and no Internet access.
