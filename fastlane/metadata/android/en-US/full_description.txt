Five calculators for the woodworking shop. Every result updates as you type, with a dimensioned drawing.

<b>Tile layout</b>: lay identical pieces over a surface (tile, boards, flooring, pavers, sheets). Number of whole and cut pieces, cut sizes, waste percentage, stacked or offset joints, gaps and perimeter gap.

<b>Spacing</b>: space pieces across a width (balusters, slats, shelves, drilling centers). From a count or a target gap, with margins, and the pitch to mark out.

<b>Drawers</b>: from the cabinet opening, the cut list for the boxes and fronts, the slide length and where to screw the slides.

<b>Level</b>: bubble level and inclinometer using the phone's accelerometer, with zeroing.

<b>Converter</b>: lengths, areas, volumes, weights and pressures, limited to the units you meet on a job site. Inches in fractions down to 1/16, board feet.

Each calculation's drawing exports as an image, to your photos or through sharing.

Everything is in millimeters. In French and English. No ads, no account, and no Internet access.
